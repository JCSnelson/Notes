Data Protection Act 2018:
Information used fairly, lawfully and transparently, explicit purposes.
Used only as necessary and relevant
Accurate data
Kept for no longer than is necessary

Stronger protection for sensitive info (e.g. info used for discrimination)


You have the right to access data and be informed, to correct data and have it erased and to restrict the processing of your data.

Regulatory of Investigatory Powers Act 2000:
regulates electronic surveillance (cameras) and investigation by public bodies
limits of police and investigative powers and responsibilities of companies.
Police can, demand access to ISP customers digital communications, without informing them. Carry out mass surveillance of digital communications.
Demand keys to encrypted information
keep secret the existence of interception warrants and data collected, even in court.


Compute Misuse Act 1990  (2015):
illegal to break into a computer system or change data without permission aswell as creating tools for such purposes.
